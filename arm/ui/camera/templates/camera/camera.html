Tuning tips
  • Trays take one to two seconds to retract. Use Test capture on a drive with its tray open, then check the photo in the gallery: a half-closed tray means raise the lead time, an empty tray means lower it.
  • Keep CAMERA_BUFFER_SECONDS comfortably above the lead time.
  • Only the CAMERA_* and DISC_PHOTO_* keys are written; the rest of arm.yaml is left untouched. The daemon reloads them within a second or two, no restart needed.
